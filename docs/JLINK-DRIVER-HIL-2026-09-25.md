This establishes that an immediate driver switch is not required to reproduce the current SWD failure. It does not identify the electrical or target-state cause and does not rule out a separate short-lived post-switch fault. Because the direct-connection baseline is already 0/10, an immediate-after-switch comparison would not be interpretable. Keep the fast path unchanged for now and investigate SWDIO/SWCLK/GND/VTref continuity, board power and target state on the dedicated setup. Once the direct-connection baseline is working, repeat the paired baseline/after-switch protocol in `test/hil/README.md`. Do not treat a single IDR failure as a reason for automatic driver rollback.
